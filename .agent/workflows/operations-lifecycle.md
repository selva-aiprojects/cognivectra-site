description: How to manage the end-to-end lifecycle of employees, clients, projects, and offers.
---

### 1. Generating an Offer
1. Navigate to the **Offers Manager** at `/admin/offers`.
2. Click on **+ New Offer**.
3. Select a candidate and link them to an active job from the **Job Board** (`/admin/jobs`).
4. Choose a pre-defined **Compensation Package** (managed at `/admin/compensation`).
5. Review the generated offer letter and send it to `selvakumar.b@cognivectra.com` for final approval before dispatching to the candidate.

### 2. Client Onboarding (CRM)
1. Go to the **CRM / Client Relations** module at `/admin/clients`.
2. For new leads, review the **Lead Reports** at `/admin/reports` first to assess the lead score (Hot/Warm).
3. Click **Add Client** in the CRM.
4. Enter company details, including the primary contact and industry.
5. Set the status to `Onboarding` to trigger the initial setup phase.

### 3. Project Generation & Management
1. Open the **Project Board** at `/admin/projects`.
2. Click **+ New Project**.
3. Select the newly onboarded **Client** from the dropdown menu.
4. Define the project scope (e.g., *Startup Launch Pack*, *AI Automation Sprint*).
5. Set the budget, start date, and assign the lead architect.
6. Updates to project metrics will reflect automatically on the **Command Center** dashboard (`/admin`).

### 4. Employee & Talent Management
1. Once an offer is accepted, navigate to **Hiring & Talent** at `/admin/jobs`.
2. Update the candidate's status to `Hired`.
3. This will automatically update the **Total Staff** metric in the `Admin` dashboard.
4. For periodic compensation reviews, use the **Compensation Table** at `/admin/compensation` to ensure alignment with industry benchmarks.

### 5. Oversight & Reporting
- Use the **Lead Reports** (`/admin/reports`) to monitor the conversion rate from Chatbot leads to Clients.
- Check the **Command Center** (`/admin`) for a high-level view of business health, pending payments, and project progress.
- All critical approvals and notifications should be routed to `selvakumar.b@cognivectra.com`.
