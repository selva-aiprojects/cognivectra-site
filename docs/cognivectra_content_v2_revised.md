# Cognivectra Website V2 --- Revised Content & Product Portfolio

## Document Purpose

This is the revised content and positioning specification for the
Cognivectra website.

This version incorporates the clarified product ownership and brand
relationship:

-   **Cognivectra** is the technology, engineering and product/IP
    organization.
-   **Cybelinx** is a market-facing enterprise product portfolio/brand
    built using Cognivectra technology and products.
-   Selected Cognivectra products are rebranded under the Cybelinx
    **Cybe** naming convention.
-   Some Cognivectra products retain their original names.
-   Product lineage should be shown clearly where a Cybelinx product is
    a rebrand or portfolio version of an existing Cognivectra product.
-   Do not present Cognivectra and Cybelinx as unrelated competing
    companies.

This is a **content, positioning and conversion refinement**, not a
visual redesign. Preserve Cognivectra's current premium, futuristic and
technical visual identity.
